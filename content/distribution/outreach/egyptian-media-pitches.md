# Egyptian & MENA Media Outreach Pack

**Why this and not mass-posting.** Placing your name on unrelated Egyptian sites
is link spam, and Google's link-spam and scaled-content-abuse policies penalise
the *target* domain — yours. Editorial coverage on a real outlet is the opposite:
it's an earned mention on a high-authority Egyptian domain, it's the kind of
citation that builds a Knowledge Graph entity, and it can't be filtered out as
spam because it isn't.

These pitch as **you**, from your own address. Send them yourself — I can draft
but shouldn't email on your behalf.

## Pitch B — the research angle (Waya, Egypt Innovate, Enterprise)

> **Subject:** An Egyptian researcher's null result on predicting in-game spending — and why it matters
>
> Hi [name],
>
> I'm Zyad Husseini — Egyptian, AUC economics graduate, currently completing an
> MSc in Data Analytics at Kedge Business School in France.
>
> My thesis tried to predict which players spend money in games, using five
> public datasets covering 50,000+ players. Thirteen classifiers all landed at
> chance — ROC AUC 0.48 to 0.52. Rather than tune until something stuck, I built
> a control: the same pipeline hit 0.95 ROC AUC on genuine game-level data.
>
> That turns "my models failed" into a defensible claim: the behavioural signal
> is missing from public data, not from the world. Which means the researchers
> best placed to audit behaviour-based monetisation are the ones least able to.
>
> There's also a separate econometrics paper of mine that found renewable energy
